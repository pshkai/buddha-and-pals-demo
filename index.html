01 / A PLACE WITH CHARACTER

Old walls.
New memories.

Tucked into the historic Nang Loeng neighbourhood, Buddha & Pals brings coffee, conversation and live music into the same story.

Part of Kanvela House on Krung Kasem Road, the café and jazz bar pairs old Bangkok’s architectural character with an easy reason to linger: something in your cup, something on your plate, and music to settle into.
