## Digital opportunity assessment

Strengths: distinctive old-city architecture, recognizable café-to-jazz identity, established social destinations, and reported live-music appeal. Likely audiences include Bangkok café visitors, jazz listeners, couples, friends and travellers; this is an inference from venue positioning, not customer demographic data.

Three main customer-friction opportunities: essential visit information is distributed across sources with conflicting hours; current menus/entry conditions are hard to establish from accessible sources; customers need a clear route from inspiration to contacting the team. Direct official social pages were inaccessible to the research tool, so no unsupported claims about their actual mobile usability or performance are made. No standalone official website was established in the accessible results; this does not prove none exists.

The proposed site brings the two experiences into one mobile-friendly story, gives immediate phone/directions/social actions, and demonstrates a low-friction visit planner. Its warm green, cream, aged brass and burgundy direction is inspired by published descriptions of the vintage building and interior. This is an interpretation, not a verified official brand palette. A typographic name treatment is used, not an invented official logo.

Owner value: a coherent, shareable starting point that makes the venue understandable to new guests and makes contact easier. No sales or booking improvements are guaranteed. Real photography, accurate current menus/hours and the official reservation channel would make the production version stronger. Competitor cloning was unnecessary; the documented identity provided sufficient direction.

## Feedback handling

A brief, subjective synthesis links to original Tripadvisor and Maps destinations. Cross-platform guest themes include live music and vintage atmosphere; some mention high drink costs and dietary limitations. No review text, invented reviewer identities, stars, review counts, credentials or awards are reproduced. No Google widget is simulated.

## Artwork & font credits

- `assets/jazz-concept.webp`: original AI-generated concept using the built-in ImageGen tool, optimized from its PNG output. It depicts a fictional interior, not the real venue. The image is explicitly labeled on the site. No social photos or third-party venue photography were copied.
- Final generation prompt: “Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-bleed website hero artwork for an independent Bangkok cafe and jazz bar concept. Create a richly atmospheric painterly editorial illustration, wide landscape 3:2 composition. A fictional old Bangkok shophouse jazz cafe at night, tall aged teal wooden windows on left, weathered plaster and exposed brick, warm amber chandelier, empty round wooden cafe tables in foreground, upright bass and piano on an intimate stage to the right, velvet burgundy curtains, soft lamplight, deep espresso shadows. Elegant cinematic oil-paint texture, restrained nostalgic green, cognac and ochre palette. No text, no logos, no people. This is evocative concept artwork, not a depiction of an actual venue. Detailed and sophisticated, not cartoonish.”
- Small café/jazz and neighbourhood graphics are original CSS illustrations. Map illustration is explicitly not to scale; Maps link provides actual navigation.
- DM Sans and Playfair Display are served via Google Fonts (open font licenses). System fallbacks preserve readability if unavailable. Google Fonts is an external request, not a paid dependency.
